What is Tajweed? (by stage)

Stage 1 (beginner): Tajweed means saying every Arabic letter from its own place in the mouth or throat, holding long sounds 2 counts, and reciting slowly and clearly.

Stage 2: Tajweed (root j‑w‑d, “to make better”) is giving each letter its right (makhraj, ṣifāt) and its due (ghunnah, idghām, madd). Allah says: وَرَتِّلِ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَرْتِيلًا (73:4).

Stage 3: Scholars generally say avoiding clear mistakes is required and subtle ones should be avoided with effort. Learn by talaqqī from a qualified teacher.

Colours: silent madd 2 madd longer qalqalah ghunnah/ikhfā’/iqlāb idghām
