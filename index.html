·

How to pronounce

Arabic has 28 letters (plus hamza ء, which sits on a seat such as أ ؤ ئ). Some letters are heavy (emphatic): ص ض ط ظ and غ خ ق, said with the back of the tongue pulled back and the mouth full. ح and ع come from the middle of the throat, while ه is a light breath. Short vowels: fatḥah (a), kasrah (i), ḍammah (u). Letters like ا و ي can be long vowels.

Voice tools need Chrome, Edge or Safari, and a microphone. The speech engine on your device is not a qari, so also listen to a trusted reciter.

LEARN · PRACTISE · MASTER

Tajweed Academy

Understand the pronunciation rules used in Quran recitation. Start with the fundamentals and practise with a qualified teacher.

01

What is Tajweed?

Tajweed means giving each Arabic letter its proper pronunciation and observing the rules of recitation. Learn the articulation points (makhārij) and characteristics (ṣifāt).

Foundation
02

Vowels & Madd

Learn fatḥah, kasrah, ḍammah and sukūn. Madd means lengthening sounds; the length depends on the specific rule.

Beginner
03

Ghunnah & Nūn Rules

Study the nasal sound (ghunnah) and the rules of iẓhār, idghām, iqlāb and ikhfā’ for nūn sākinah and tanwīn.

Intermediate
04

Qalqalah & Waqf

Recognise the echoing articulation of ق ط ب ج د in the appropriate conditions, and learn where to pause and resume recitation.

Intermediate
